In select_runnable_task (zbobr-dispatcher/src/cli.rs ~line 302), add a filter condition to exclude tasks where `t.state.is_ready() && !t.stack.is_empty()`. These tasks are handled by `apply_ready_from_state` in the loop's Phase 1 and then deferred (continue) — never added to runstage_candidates.
